# Decision-Making Process

1. **Routine Documentation & Typo Fixes:** Reviewed and approved by one maintainer or relevant Language Lead after passing CI hygiene checks.
2. **Linguistic Standards & Regional Orthography Rulings:** Decided via transparent discussion with relevant Language Leads and Cultural Advisors, favoring community consensus and documenting regional coexistence when legitimate variants exist.
3. **Schema Versioning, Licensing & Security Policies:** Decided solely by Divinari core maintainers with explicit semantic versioning guarantees (`divinari-developer-resources`).
