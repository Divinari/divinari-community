# Community Onboarding

Welcome to the **Divinari** community! Whether you are a native speaker, linguist, pastor, university instructor, NGO coordinator, or developer, there is a clear path to participate.

## Ways to Get Involved

1. **Join Community Conversations:** Connect via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Divinari/divinari-community/discussions) or our Discord server (`https://discord.gg/gr3mXDAXPt`).
2. **Review or Propose Linguistic Content:** Start with [Native Reviewer Handbook](../volunteer-guidelines/native-reviewer-handbook.md) and open requests in `divinari-language-resources` (once target language tracks open).
3. **Explore Open Schemas & Validators:** Developers and computational linguists can inspect public schemas in [`Divinari/divinari-developer-resources`](https://github.com/Divinari/divinari-developer-resources).
4. **Propose an Institutional, Academic, or Ministry Collaboration:** Review [Partnership Principles](../partnerships/partnership-principles.md) and open a [Partnership Inquiry](https://github.com/Divinari/divinari-community/issues/new?template=partnership-inquiry.yml).

## Ground Rules

- Follow our [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and DCO sign-off requirements ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Never share third-party copyrighted material, private user info, or unverified bulk AI outputs.
