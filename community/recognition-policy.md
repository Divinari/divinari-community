# Contributor Recognition Policy

We believe linguistic contributors and cultural advisors deserve clear, lasting public credit.

## How Contributions Are Credited

- **Attribution Registry:** Accepted contributors to `divinari-language-resources` are credited in `attribution/CONTRIBUTORS.md` using their chosen display name or GitHub handle, language(s), and scope of review.
- **Commercial App & Web Attribution (CC BY 4.0):** Whenever community-contributed `CC BY 4.0` entries are incorporated into Divinari's commercial mobile app (`divinari.app`) or web platform, Divinari includes an open-resource attribution notice linking back to the public repository release and contributors record.
- **Clear Separation from Paid Tiers:** Volunteer recognition does not imply employment, equity, or automatic paid subscriptions unless explicitly offered via a separate community scholarship or pilot agreement.
