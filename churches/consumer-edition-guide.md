# Consumer Faith & Heritage Access Guide

In the consumer Divinari platform, **public-domain Scripture reading tools remain 100% free** without paywalling access to verified Scripture texts, while advanced AI tutoring and premium commercial features sustain ongoing development.

- **Public-Domain Standard:** Public ecosystem repositories only accept verified public-domain or cleanly licensed open Scripture metadata (e.g., documented 1905/1909 public-domain editions or World English Bible with explicit provenance metadata)—never unauthorized modern copyrighted translations.
- **No Coercion:** Learners who prefer purely cultural, academic, or secular tracks can use Divinari without faith-specific exercises where secular variants apply.
