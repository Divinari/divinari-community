# Church & Fellowship Language Programs

Divinari equips congregations, bilingual fellowships, and heritage families to connect across generations through shared language and Scripture.

## Supporting Diaspora & Bilingual Congregations

- **Intergenerational Connection:** Support younger diaspora speakers reconnecting with parents' and grandparents' languages (such as Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano, or Spanish) alongside verified public-domain Bible study tracks.
- **Hospitality & Local Outreach:** Prepare newcomers and outreach teams with natural conversational phrases, cultural humility, and respectful register usage (`po`/`opo`, kinship terms, community greetings).
- **Review Contributions Welcome:** Bilingual congregation leaders can apply via our [Volunteer Reviewer Form](https://github.com/Divinari/divinari-community/issues/new?template=volunteer-reviewer-application.yml) to help audit naturalness and respectful liturgical terminology.
