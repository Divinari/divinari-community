# Indigenous Data Ethics & AI Training Transparency

Divinari commits to aligning community language curation with the **CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** (*Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics*):

1. **Collective Benefit & Non-Mislabeling:** Respect distinct linguistic identities and orthographies across Philippine (including Mindanao, Cordillera, and Visayan) and Indigenous Americas communities.
2. **Authority to Control:** Restricted ceremonial, sacred, or elder-custodied oral traditions must **not** be submitted to open GitHub repositories or commercial datasets without documented authorization from recognized community custodians.
3. **Transparent AI Training Disclosure:** Divinari actively develops open-weight-derived AI language tutoring adapters alongside its educational app. Because `CC BY 4.0` permits computational text processing with attribution, any public repository contribution may inform educational search or linguistic tutor tooling **unless** explicitly segregated under a restricted cultural notice. Communities or partners seeking strict AI-training exclusion for sensitive collections must highlight this up front before submitting data so separate non-AI handling can be arranged.
