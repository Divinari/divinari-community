# Divinari Language Heritage Charter

1. **Every Language Has Inherent Worth:** Regional and Indigenous languages are living cultural treasures—not "dialects" or footnotes to global trade languages.
2. **Native Speakers Lead Quality:** Fluency and cultural authenticity cannot be judged by algorithms alone; native and heritage speakers hold final say on natural register and idiomatic meaning.
3. **Separation of Open Heritage vs. Commercial Packaging:** Core linguistic preservation contributions published in `divinari-language-resources` stay openly accessible under `CC BY 4.0` with public contributor credit.
