# University & Academic Research Collaboration

Divinari welcomes collaboration with departments of linguistics, Philippine studies, Indigenous studies, language pedagogy, and computer science.

## Shared Research Interests

- **Cross-Linguistic Concept Mapping:** Evaluating language-independent Concept Hub representations across Austronesian focus/aspect systems, Romance languages, and Indigenous grammars.
- **Script Digitization & Stroke Pedagogies:** Documenting normalized `[0.0, 1.0]` vector stroke definitions for historical writing systems (`Baybayin`, `Badlit`, `Kulitan`, `Kur-itan`, etc.).
- **CEFR & ACTFL Alignment:** Validating transparent Can-Do progression rubrics for low-resource and heritage languages.
- **Honest Infrastructure Scope:** Please note that classroom cohort management features are in prototype phase, and existing raw commercial database exports undergo strict cleaning before academic publication.
