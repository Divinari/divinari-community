# Audio Contributor Guide (Phase 5 Preview — Submissions Currently Closed)

> **Status:** Public audio Pull Requests are **currently blocked by CI** while Phase 5 infrastructure and legal releases are finalized. Text, IPA, and syllabic stress contributions remain open.

## Planned Recording Technical Standard

- **Format:** Uncompressed `WAV` (`PCM`), `48 kHz`, `24-bit`, `Mono`
- **Acoustic Environment:** Quiet room (`<-60 dBFS` noise floor), minimal room echo, zero clipping or background music
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Output from any text-to-speech engine, neural voice synthesizer, or cloned speaker voice

## Mandatory Legal Precondition

When Phase 5 audio intake opens, every spoken submission must accompany a signed **Voice Contributor Release** explicitly consenting to `CC BY 4.0` distribution and clarifying permitted/prohibited acoustic model uses.
