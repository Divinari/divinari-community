# Translation & Anti-Literalism Standards

1. **Translate Meaning, Not Word Order:** Natural Tagalog, Cebuano, or Hiligaynon focus/aspect phrasing (`kumain`, `kinain`, `nakakain`) must never be forced into English syntax.
2. **Zero Synthetic Fillers:** Reject generic filler patterns (`"... plays a vital role in ..."`, numbered headword placeholders, or untranslated English words dropped inside target-language headwords).
3. **Contextual Examples:** Every example sentence must demonstrate believable human speech or writing with natural collocates.
4. **Honor Regional Variations Explicitly:** Mark regional usage cleanly (e.g., Batangas vs. Metro Manila Tagalog; Cebuano vs. Mindanawon Bisaya) without erasing local identity.
