# Native Reviewer & Language Lead Handbook

Thank you for safeguarding linguistic quality for learners and future generations!

## Reviewer Responsibilities

1. **Authenticity First:** Confirm words, idioms, and sample sentences sound like genuine everyday or formal usage from fluent speakers—reject stiff literalism or artificial template sentences.
2. **Verify Polysemy & Register:** Ensure distinct meanings sit in separate sense records (`word_senses`) and register labels (`formal`, `casual`, `regional`, `liturgical`, `archaic`) match reality.
3. **Check Provenance Strictly:** Confirm every PR names its origin (`native_speaker`, `public_domain`, or `cc_by_4_0` with citation). Immediately reject entries copied from copyrighted modern dictionaries, textbooks, or commercial news sites.
4. **Verify Token Spans & IPA:** Confirm syllabic stress, IPA notation, and example-sentence word indices match the surface text cleanly (verified automatically by CI as well).
