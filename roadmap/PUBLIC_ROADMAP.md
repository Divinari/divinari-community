# Public Ecosystem Roadmap

> Dates and phase targets represent good-faith community milestones and may adjust based on native-reviewer capacity and funding.

## Phase 1 — Community & Governance Foundation *(Active)*
- Launch clean-room governance, inclusivity standards, reviewer handbooks, and partnership intake workflows in `divinari-community`.

## Phase 2 — Public Developer Specifications *(Active)*
- Publish Draft 2020-12 JSON Schemas (`lexicon-entry`, `example-sentence`, `writing-system`, `language-metadata`, `curriculum-unit`, `heritage-contribution`, `scripture-bundle`) and standalone Python validator in `divinari-developer-resources`.

## Phase 3 — Cleared Tagalog Pilot & Language Scaffolds
- Populate initial native-reviewed `CC BY 4.0` Tagalog (`tl`) starter lexicon and clean-room Baybayin writing-system definitions in `divinari-language-resources`.
- Recruit Language Leads for Cebuano (`ceb`), Hiligaynon (`hil`), and Ilocano (`ilo`).

## Phase 4 — Verified Native Audio & Expanded Preservation
- Open verified `48 kHz / 24-bit WAV` native speaker recording workflows once signed Voice Contributor Releases and storage hosting are in place.
- Expand verified regional, Indigenous, and public-domain Scripture formatting tracks.
