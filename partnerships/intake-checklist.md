# Partnership Intake Checklist

Before beginning a formal collaboration or pilot with Divinari:

- [ ] **Scope Defined:** Clarify target languages, curriculum vs. preservation output, and timeline.
- [ ] **Track Confirmed:** Specify secular/institutional alignment vs. optional Scripture/faith-integrated study.
- [ ] **IP & Licensing Agreed:** Confirm outputs intended for public `CC BY 4.0` or `Apache 2.0` vs. private internal evaluation.
- [ ] **Cultural & Community Consent:** Confirm local elders, community leads, or ethics boards approve any heritage or Indigenous materials shared.
- [ ] **Designated Contacts:** Name primary coordinators on both sides and confirm written consent before any public announcement.
