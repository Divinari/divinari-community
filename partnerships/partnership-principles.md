# Partnership Principles

Divinari builds long-term collaborations with universities, NGOs, heritage organizations, churches, and field educators anchored in four core principles:

1. **No Implied Endorsement:** Informal discussions, issue threads, or open-source PRs do **not** establish official partnership status. No organization name or logo may appear in Divinari marketing or documentation without signed mutual authorization.
2. **Non-Extractive Heritage Stewardship:** Community knowledge remains openly accessible under `CC BY 4.0` where shared publicly, honoring attribution and CARE Indigenous Data Governance principles.
3. **Clear Commercial vs. Open Boundaries:** Divinari sustains long-term platform engineering through its proprietary app and commercial tiers (`divinari.app`), while open linguistic resources and developer schemas remain permanently open for preservation and research.
4. **Honest Scope Disclosure:** Experimental or pilot institutional features are always presented candidly with clear timelines and limitations.
