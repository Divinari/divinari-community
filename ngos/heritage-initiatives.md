# NGO & Heritage Preservation Initiatives

Cultural NGOs, diaspora community centers, and language revitalization teams can partner with Divinari to document endangered scripts, regional vocabulary, and authentic usage guides in open, reusable formats.

- **Open Preservation Output:** Vetted linguistic entries and script stroke specifications live in public `CC BY 4.0` and `Apache 2.0` repositories (`divinari-language-resources` and `divinari-developer-resources`) so partner communities always retain open access to the published corpus.
- **Community Authority:** Heritage organizations decide which cultural topics are appropriate for open documentation and which traditional knowledge must remain private to the community.
