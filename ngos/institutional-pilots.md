# Institutional & Secular Pilots (Current Scope Disclosure)

Divinari supports exploration with NGOs, schools, and secular heritage programs. In the spirit of full transparency:

- **Prototype / Pilot Status:** Dedicated secular institutional branding and multi-tenant classroom LMS dashboards are currently **under active internal development / pilot hardening** rather than general-availability enterprise SaaS.
- **What Can Start Today:** Organizations can collaborate immediately on **curriculum terminology vetting**, **public CC BY 4.0 lexicon curation**, **writing-system documentation**, and **academic corpus research** using `divinari-developer-resources` and `divinari-language-resources`.
