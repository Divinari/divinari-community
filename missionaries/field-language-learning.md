# Ethical Field Language Learning

Long-term cross-cultural workers and humanitarian teams learn best when language study flows from humility, listening, and genuine respect for host communities.

- **Prioritize Conversational & Cultural Literacy:** Start with everyday listening, local proverbs, family terms, and regional variants rather than isolated translated slogans.
- **Honor Regional Languages:** In regions like the Philippines or Latin America, learn the heart language actually spoken in your host community (e.g., Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano, Bikol, Quechua, Guarani) rather than assuming national capitals speak for every region.
