# Cultural Humility & Respectful Collaboration Guidelines

1. **No Unsolicited Evangelism in Community Spaces:** Shared linguistic PRs, GitHub Discussions, and language-preservation channels exist for collaborative linguistic accuracy and mutual respect.
2. **Respect Community Consent & Non-Disclosure:** Never extract sacred stories, private community ceremonies, or confidential personal narratives into public or commercial datasets without explicit informed consent from community custodians.
3. **Reciprocal Credit:** Recognize local language teachers and native reviewers prominently in attribution records rather than presenting community wisdom as solely outside authorship.
