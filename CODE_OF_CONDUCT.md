# Divinari Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, linguists, educators, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Divinari community a harassment-free, welcoming, and dignified experience for everyone—regardless of language background, ethnicity, nationality, faith tradition or secular worldview, age, disability, socioeconomic status, sex, or level of academic training.

## Core Community Commitments

1. **Linguistic & Cultural Dignity:** Treat every language and community heritage with respect. Never disparage regional or minority languages (such as Bisaya/Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano, Bikol, Maranao, Tausug, or Indigenous languages of the Americas) as "broken" or "inferior dialects."
2. **Pluralism & Mutual Respect:** Divinari serves learners across both secular educational settings and Christian faith communities. Respect every contributor's beliefs—never exert proselytizing pressure in community spaces, and never belittle sincere faith-heritage learning.
3. **Intellectual Honesty & Consent:** Respect community ownership over sacred or restricted cultural narratives, and never copy proprietary third-party materials into open contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, derogatory remarks, cultural stereotyping, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information (doxxing) without explicit permission
- Submitting deliberately synthetic, unverified machine-translated, or plagiarized linguistic content
- Misrepresenting partnership or endorsement by external institutions or elders

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **support@divinari.app** (subject: `Code of Conduct Report`) or contact repository maintainers directly. All reports are reviewed confidentially with proportional corrective action ranging from private coaching to temporary or permanent removal from community spaces.
